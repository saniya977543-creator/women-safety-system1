Awareness and preparation

Self-defence & personal safety

Your safety comes first. These general tips are not a substitute for professional training or emergency support.

01

Notice your surroundings

When practical, stay aware of nearby exits, open businesses, people, and places where you could ask for help.

02

Set a clear boundary

If it feels safe, use a firm voice and move toward other people or a staffed public place.

03

Prioritise getting away

Focus on creating an opportunity to reach safety and contact someone you trust or emergency services.

04

Learn with a qualified instructor

Consider a reputable, in-person personal-safety course that emphasises awareness, boundaries, and escape.

In immediate danger? Move to a safer public location if possible and call 112 in India.
