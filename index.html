Safety tips

Use the steps that feel safe and appropriate for your situation.

Before travelling

Plan your route, keep your phone charged, and consider letting someone you trust know your plans.

If you feel unsafe

Move toward a populated, well-lit place if possible. Contact someone you trust or call 112 for immediate emergency assistance.

Keep records safely

If it is safe, note relevant dates and details. Store any records somewhere only you can access.

Ask for support

Reach out to a trusted person, a local support organisation, or an appropriate helpline. You deserve to be heard.

Know your rights

Everyone deserves dignity, safety, and equal treatment. For legal advice or help with a specific situation, contact a qualified legal professional or a local support organisation.

Frequently asked questions

Does this website send SOS alerts?

No. This is a static HTML and CSS website. Use the call buttons to contact emergency services directly.

Does this site track my location?

No. It does not collect or track your location. Map links open an external map search when you choose them.
