Safe route planning

Search for a destination in a map service, then review the route and transport options before you leave.

Your search opens Google Maps in a new tab. Do not enter a private home address on a shared device.

Before you go

Check the route, transport availability, approximate travel time, and whether the destination is open.

Stay connected

If it feels safe, let someone you trust know your plan and when you expect to arrive.

Choose public options

Consider staffed transport points and familiar public places. Reassess if conditions change.

Emergency support

If you are in immediate danger in India, call 112.

SafetyHer does not calculate, verify, or monitor routes. Map results and conditions may change; use your judgement.
